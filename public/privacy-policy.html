Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Crunchy Living ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name to provide our services.

Camera Access: The app uses your device camera solely to scan product barcodes and ingredient labels. Images captured during scanning are processed in real-time and are not stored on our servers.

Product Data: When you save products, we store product names, ingredients, and analysis results in your account so you can access them later.

Quiz Responses: Your crunchy score quiz answers are stored to personalize your experience and recommendations.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide product ingredient analysis and health scores
  • Save your scanned products and preferences
  • Personalize DIY recipe recommendations
  • Improve our product database and analysis accuracy

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: For secure data storage and authentication
  • Google Gemini API: For ingredient analysis (only ingredient text is sent, never personal information)

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We only share data with service providers necessary to operate the app.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure authentication to protect your data.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time through the app settings. Upon deletion, all your data is permanently removed from our servers.

Children's Privacy

Crunchy Living is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@crunchyliving.app
